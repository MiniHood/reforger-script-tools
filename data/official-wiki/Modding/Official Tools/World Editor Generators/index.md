Generators, as their name suggests, are entities that generate *something* (forest, road, wall, river, powerlines etc).

They are based on Shapes, polylines/splines, closed or not depending on the generator. As of Arma Reforger 1.0.0, a generator **must** be a shape's direct child.

ⓘ

* For information on generator usage, see [World Editor Generators tutorials](/wiki/Category:Arma_Reforger/Modding/Official_Tools/Workbench/World_Editor_Generators/Tutorials "Category:Arma Reforger/Modding/Official Tools/Workbench/World Editor Generators/Tutorials").
* For information on shapes, see [Vector Tool](/wiki/Arma_Reforger:World_Editor:_Vector_Tool "Arma Reforger:World Editor: Vector Tool").
