# [Field Manual](https://community.bistudio.com/wiki/Arma_Reforger:Field_Manual)

**RTFM**

**R**ead **T**he **F**ield **M**anual.

The **Field Manual** is the in-game manual providing detailed, illustrated information about game modes ([Conflict](/wiki/Arma_Reforger:Conflict "Arma Reforger:Conflict"), [Game Master](/wiki/Arma_Reforger:Game_Master "Arma Reforger:Game Master")), gameplay (movement, commanding, etc), and equipment.

It is accessible through **Main Menu > Field Manual** or **Pause Menu > Field Manual**.

See also [Field Manual Modding](/wiki/Arma_Reforger:Field_Manual_Modding "Arma Reforger:Field Manual Modding").
