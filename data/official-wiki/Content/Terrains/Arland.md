« A small overseas territory, Arland officially belongs to the microstate of [Everon](/wiki/Arma_Reforger:Everon "Arma Reforger:Everon").

Although remote and sparsely populated, this link in the Malden Islands chain is tempered by the presence of a major Soviet airbase — putting it at the forefront of Cold War tensions.
Beyond the razor wire fences, close-knit communities can also be found. Arleville and Beauregard. Here, Czech-speaking locals carry on as they have for hundreds of years.

Toiling the sea and working the land, attentive to the natural beauty that surrounds them. » – [Arma Reforger website](https://reforger.armaplatform.com/game)
