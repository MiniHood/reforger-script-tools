**Everon** is an island of the Malden archipelago. It was historically [first introduced](/wiki/Everon "Everon") in [Operation Flashpoint](/wiki/Category:Operation_Flashpoint "Category:Operation Flashpoint") and is revisited in [Arma Reforger](/wiki/Category:Arma_Reforger "Category:Arma Reforger").

Its landscape is based on the Croatian island of [Krk](https://en.wikipedia.org/Krk).

«

« Part of a Mid-Atlantic archipelago, this independent nation gained international attention in 1985 when a rogue Soviet general staged an unsuccessful invasion. Although a small and tranquil island, quietly pursuing its own socialist ideals, it remains a geostrategic prize and home to a permanent US garrison. Following years of peace, Everon awaits the consequences of a war, arising beyond its shores. Torn between the machinations of world powers, it is only a matter of time before the first expeditionary force arrives and returns the so-called 'Naval of the Sea' to a state of bitter conflict. » – [Arma Reforger website](https://reforger.armaplatform.com/game)
