**Kolguyev** (Kolgujev (CZ), Колгу́ев (RU), Kolguyev (EN)) is an island of the Malden archipelago and part of the Soviet Union's territory. It was historically [first introduced](/wiki/Kolgujev "Kolgujev") in [Operation Flashpoint](/wiki/Category:Operation_Flashpoint "Category:Operation Flashpoint") and is revisited in [Arma Reforger](/wiki/Category:Arma_Reforger "Category:Arma Reforger").

Its landscape is based on [Tenerife](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tenerife) island, while its name is based on the real-life [Kolguyev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolguyev_Island) island in the Barents Sea.

ⓘ

See also the first [Kolgu*j*ev](/wiki/Kolgujev "Kolgujev") from 2001's Operation Flashpoint.

« Kolguyev is a small landmass around 300 km north of the Malden archipelago, its dominant feature being a dormant volcanic mountain peak.A territory of the Soviet Union, Kolguyev has suffered decades of severe environmental degradation from mining operations and is now virtually deserted, its settlements long abandoned.All that remains is a strong Soviet military garrison due to the strategic value of the island on the geopolitical stage. » – [Arma Reforger website](https://reforger.armaplatform.com/game)
