## Start with the Game

Quickstart Guide for Arma Reforger

* Read the [Boot Camp](https://reforger.armaplatform.com/news/boot-camp) article - useful both for beginners' boarding and veteran users to be up-to-date on the game's many aspects!
* Read the in-game [Field Manual](/wiki/Arma_Reforger:Field_Manual "Arma Reforger:Field Manual") (**Main Menu > Field Manual** or **Pause Menu > Field Manual**)
* Set your Settings:
  + Set your graphical preferences (**Main Menu > Settings > Video**)
  + Set your controls (**Main Menu > Settings > Controls**)

## Play in Single Player

### Tutorial

The tutorial is available from the main menu through **Play > Tutorial** (scrolling through the entries may be required).

## Playing in Multiplayer

The **Multiplayer** main menu tile offers an interface to choose from various servers - from Official to Community-hosted ones.

## Use Mods

The **Workshop** main menu tile opens the [Workshop](/wiki/Arma_Reforger:Workshop "Arma Reforger:Workshop") that hosts all game-accessible community-created content - missions, assets, features.

Download and activate mods from the Workshop to expand the game's possibilities!
